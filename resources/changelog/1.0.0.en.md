TáborPlanner 1.0 is here — everything you need to plan a summer camp, in one place.

## Plan your days

- A planner grid for the whole camp: click a slot to add an activity, drag to move it.
- Days share one time skeleton you edit under **Blocks**, with per-day exceptions where a day runs differently.
- Save **plan versions** before a big change and come back to them whenever you like.
- Lock the schedule once it is final, and duplicate the whole camp next year.

## Activity library

- Keep your activities in reusable libraries, sorted with your own categories.
- Share a library with other organisers by link, or export and import it as a file.
- Library versions back up the whole collection before you reorganise it.

## Your team

- Invite leaders by e-mail or share link, and assign them to groups.
- Split the camp into groups — teams, programme, photographers — with your own group types.

## Points and feedback

- Mark an activity as scoring and record how the groups placed; the leaderboard keeps the running standings.
- Leaders rate each day with stars in the evening and answer your camp's own feedback questions.
- The feedback page collects every review in one place.

## AI summaries

- Generate a summary of a single day or of the whole camp with Gemini.
- Bring your own API key — it is stored encrypted and used only for your camp.

## Your account

- Two-factor authentication and passkeys.
- Export all your data at any time.
- Full Slovak and English throughout.
